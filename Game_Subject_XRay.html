🌟 VERB 'TO BE' (Am / Is / Are)
State & Identity
Describes who, what, where, how someone is. No auxiliary 'do/does' ever!
(+) Affirmative S + am / is / are + Complement e.g. I am ready. / He is a doctor. / They are happy.
(-) Negative S + am not / isn't / aren't + Complement e.g. I am not tired. / He isn't late. / We aren't ready.
(? y/n) Question Am / Is / Are + S + Complement? e.g. Am I right? / Is he your brother? / Are they ready?
(Wh-) Wh- Question Wh- + am / is / are + S + Complement? e.g. Why am I here? / Where is he? / Who are they?
Subject (+) Affirmative (-) Negative (? y/n) Question (Wh-) Wh- Question
⚡ OTHER VERBS (Action Verbs)
Actions & Routines
Describes actions, verbs, and habits. Requires auxiliary 'Do/Does' in negative & questions!
(+) Affirmative S + V / Vs + Object e.g. I work. / He works. / They live in London.
(-) Negative S + don't / doesn't + V + Object e.g. I don't work. / He doesn't work. (V bare!)
(? y/n) Question Do / Does + S + V + Object? e.g. Do you work? / Does he work?
(Wh-) Wh- Question Wh- + do / does + S + V + Object? e.g. Why do you work? / Where does he live?
Subject (+) Affirmative (-) Negative (? y/n) Question (Wh-) Wh- Question
❌ NEVER MIX THEM:
• "Is he work here?" ❌ ➔ "Does he work here?" ✅
• "He doesn't a doctor." ❌ ➔ "He isn't a doctor." ✅
🧲 THE AUXILIARY RULE:
When doesn't or does enters, it carries the -s, leaving the verb in bare base form V (e.g. Does he work? NOT Does he works?).
